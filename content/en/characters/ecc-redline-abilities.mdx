## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most effective way to practice ECC REDLINE abilities?
The best approach is utilizing the training range to master the timing of slide cancels between Phase Ignition and Crimson Thrust. Once your muscle memory handles the animation cancels consistently, transition into unranked skirmishes to practice heat management against unpredictable human movement patterns.

### How do ECC REDLINE abilities perform against heavy crowd-control teams?
Against teams loaded with stuns, roots, and suppressive traps, Redline must play as a secondary cleanup flanker rather than an initiator. Wait until the opposing squad exhausts their high-priority control spells on your frontline tanks before using Phase Ignition to eliminate vulnerable backline targets.

### Which stat should I prioritize first when upgrading ECC REDLINE gear?
Prioritize Cooldown Reduction and Kinetic Penetration first. Having high offensive power is useless if your mobility cooldowns are too slow to keep you alive between burst rotations. Keeping Phase Ignition on a short timer guarantees both offensive agility and survival consistency.

### Can ECC REDLINE abilities break through physical shields easily?
Yes. Both Thermal Disruption and fully stacked Crimson Thrust provide native armor and shield penetration. Combining these skills allows you to strip defensive shields faster than most standard kinetic operatives in the current meta.
