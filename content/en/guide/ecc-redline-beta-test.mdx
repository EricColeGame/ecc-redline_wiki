## Hardware Optimization and System Requirements

Competitive racing and combat mechanics demand consistent frame delivery and minimal input latency. Running an early build requires overhead beyond typical retail releases, as debug logging, crash dump monitoring, and unoptimized rendering pipelines place added strain on your hardware. Prior to launching your client, optimizing your operating system and graphics settings will prevent stuttering during high-speed engagements.

Below is an overview of baseline system recommendations designed to keep your machine competitive throughout heavy stress testing.

| Component Tier | Minimum Specification | Recommended Specification | Competitive High-Refresh (144Hz+) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Processor (CPU)** | 6-Core / 12-Thread (3.2 GHz base) | 8-Core / 16-Thread (3.8 GHz boost) | Modern high-IPC 8-Core processor |
| **Memory (RAM)** | 16 GB DDR4 (Dual-Channel) | 32 GB DDR4/DDR5 | 32 GB High-Speed Low-Latency DDR5 |
| **Graphics Card (GPU)** | 6 GB VRAM (DirectX 12 support) | 8 GB–12 GB VRAM modern architecture | 16 GB VRAM enthusiast-grade GPU |
| **Storage** | 70 GB SATA SSD | 70 GB NVMe M.2 SSD | 70 GB High-Speed Gen4 NVMe SSD |
| **Network** | Broadband (15 Mbps down / 5 Mbps up) | Stable Cable/Fiber (50+ Mbps down) | Direct Gigabit Ethernet (Low Jitter) |

To maximize framerate consistency during test sessions, configure your client using the following performance adjustments:

* **Disable Background Overlays:** Third-party recording tools, performance overlays, and browser tabs consume valuable memory and cause micro-stutters during intensive physics calculations.
* **Cap Frame Rates to Monitor Refresh:** Setting your maximum frame ceiling just below your monitor's native refresh rate prevents frame pacing spikes and keeps GPU utilization stable.
* **Prioritize Low-Latency Input Modes:** Activate native GPU low-latency settings through your display driver to ensure steering inputs translate immediately on-screen.
* **Switch to Fullscreen Exclusive:** Avoid borderless windowed modes whenever possible during testing builds, as desktop composition managers can introduce input delay.

Checking compatibility on platforms like the [Steam community hub and playtest platform](https://store.steampowered.com) allows users to verify hardware configurations before committing to downloads.

## Submitting Actionable Bug Reports and Telemetry

Participating in testing means working as an active collaborator with the development team. Submitting vague complaints like "the handling feels broken" does not provide software engineers with actionable data. To make your participation in the **ECC REDLINE beta test** count, learn how to document technical faults with clear reproduction steps and relevant logs.

Engineers categorize tickets according to severity, prioritizing game-breaking exploits and hard crashes over cosmetic interface issues.

| Severity Level | Classification | Example Scenario | Required Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Blocker** | Crash to Desktop (CTD) / Softlock | Client closes immediately when launching a race | Crash log (.dmp), system diagnostics (DxDiag) |
| **Tier 2: Critical** | Gameplay Exploits / Out of Bounds | Vehicle falls through track geometry at Turn 4 | Video recording, exact input reproduction steps |
| **Tier 3: Moderate** | Mechanical / Balancing Bugs | Weapon heat does not cool down after pit stop | Reproduction rate (e.g., 3/5 times), vehicle setup |
| **Tier 4: Minor** | Cosmetic / Text Errors | Overlapping UI text in the garage customization menu | High-resolution screenshot, display resolution |

When creating a formal bug submission in the official testing portal, adhere to this standard layout:

1. **Title:** Brief summary featuring the system affected (e.g., *[Physics] Sudden loss of traction on steel bridge section*).
2. **Reproduction Steps:** Step-by-step instructions that allow QA testers to reliably reproduce the fault.
3. **Expected Result:** What should happen during normal play.
4. **Actual Result:** What happened when the bug triggered.
5. **System Configuration:** Your CPU, GPU, driver version, and control peripheral.

Providing clean reports ensures development teams can quickly locate anomalous code and deliver updates throughout the active **ECC REDLINE beta test** cycle.

### Can I stream or create video content during the ECC REDLINE beta test?
Content creation policies depend strictly on whether the specific wave operates under a non-disclosure agreement (NDA). Public open betas and designated creator closed tests generally allow streaming and recording, whereas early technical previews prohibit sharing screen captures or video clips without explicit developer permission. Always verify the watermarks and startup notices displayed on your splash screen.

### What should I do if the ECC REDLINE beta test client repeatedly crashes on startup?
First, verify your game file integrity through your platform launcher to ensure no files were corrupted during download. Next, update your graphics card drivers to the latest build, disable background third-party overlays, and make sure your system meets the minimum operating specifications outlined above.
